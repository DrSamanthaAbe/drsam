What she does

Sam founded Actually Sleeping after her own experience as the parent awake at 3 a.m. with a baby who would not sleep. She writes every client's sleep plan personally; a coaching team runs daily 1:1 check-ins, with live support on the first night and naps coached until solid. The practice has coached 700+ families as of November 2026.

The 14-Day Sleep Guarantee: "We guarantee that your baby will sleep 8–12 hours through the night within 14 days of consistent, correct implementation of your sleep plan" — with a full refund if not. Full terms.

Her pharmacy training shapes the parts of infant sleep most coaches avoid: how reflux medications, teething and fever medicines, melatonin products and a breastfeeding parent's own medications and caffeine interact with a baby's sleep. She writes and reviews the guides on learn.actuallysleeping.com.
